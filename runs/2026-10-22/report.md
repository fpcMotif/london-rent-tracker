- **Rent trend (unconfirmed for now):** I found no October 2026 London figure. - **Rent against budget:** ONS shows the Sept 2026 two-bed average rose 7.35% year on year in Southwark and 6.85% in Islington (this week's input). Growth of that size could push 2027 asking rents past £3,000.
- **Renters' Rights Act, in force:** Section 21 was abolished on 1 May 2026 and fixed terms became periodic ([Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/key-dates-confirmed-for-renters-rights-act-reforms-aYmEN6Z3S3w6), published after Royal Assent on 27 Oct 2025). Landlords may price in the lost certainty.
- **Renters' Rights Act, later phases (unconfirmed):** The landlord database and Ombudsman are expected in "late 2026" ([Paul Robinson](https://paulrobinson.co.uk/the-renters-rights-act-2025-implementation-update/)). I found no confirmed date, and no timeline for the housing-quality phase.
- **Build-to-rent supply:** Vertus's 50-60 Charter Street scheme (756 homes) is part of 1,300+ Wood Wharf homes due 2025 to early 2027 ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/london/build-to-rent/jlp-takes-on-first-london-btr-site-while-canary-wharf-ramps-up-vertus-130734), undated). That could soften Canary Wharf rents. Our scrape of Vertus is incomplete: 3 two-bed units were dropped for lack of a bathroom count.
- **Other build-to-rent areas (unconfirmed):** I found no news on new openings in Aldgate, Royal Docks, Stratford or the other listed areas. Our Greystar and Moda collections returned 0 listings.

These were weekend closures, but they show how often the lines are disrupted. The Central line also closed from Liverpool Street to Woodford/Newbury Park on 26–27 Sept ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/london/news/tfl-tube-train-closures-september-26-27-2026-092526)).

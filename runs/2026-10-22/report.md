## Risks

- **Rents at record highs.** Rightmove's Q2 2026 tracker, released 16 Jul 2026, put London's average advertised rent at £2,791 pcm, up 2.0% on the quarter and the biggest quarterly rise since 2023. Available stock fell below last year's level. [Rightmove Q2 2026](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/news/content/uploads/2026/07/Rental-Trends-Tracker-Q2-2026.pdf)
- **ONS benchmarks are rising faster than the all-London figure.** For two-bed homes in Sep 2026, Southwark is up 7.35% a year and Islington up 6.85% (from this week's tracker data). A second-hand source gives London at about 2% in the 12 months to May 2026. That is unconfirmed.
- **Little room under the cap.** Every area's maximum listing is exactly £3,000. Shoreditch/Islington's median within 30 minutes is £3,000, and Stratford's is £2,925 (this week's tracker, 22 Oct 2026).
- **Renters' Rights Act in force.** Section 21 ended on 1 May 2026, and tenancies are now periodic. Rent can rise once every 12 months, and rent in advance is limited. [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/key-dates-confirmed-for-renters-rights-act-reforms-aYmEN6Z3S3w6)
- **Later Act phases are not fully confirmed.** One source says the landlord database opens 15 Dec 2026. I could not confirm that date or the ombudsman date from a primary source. [Chancellors](https://cdn.chancellors.co.uk/about-us/news/renters-rights-act-measures-begin-may-2026/)
- **New build-to-rent supply is unconfirmed.** Vertus has launched 756 flats at Charter Street, Wood Wharf, but no opening date is given. [Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/london/build-to-rent/jlp-takes-on-first-london-btr-site-while-canary-wharf-ramps-up-vertus-130734) Greystar's Stratford Mill (205 rental homes) was expected in 2026. I found no confirmed 2027 openings, and found nothing for Aldgate or Wapping.
- **Weekend closures.** Time Out lists a Jubilee closure from Green Park to Canary Wharf and a DLR Shadwell–Tower Gateway closure on 3–4 Oct 2026. [Time Out](https://timeout.com/london/news/tfl-tube-train-closures-weekend-october-3-4-2026-100226)
- **Not confirmed:** long-term works on the Northern, Elizabeth and Central lines, and at Bank station for 2027. I found nothing current. Check TfL's planned works pages.

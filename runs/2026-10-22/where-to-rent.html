What sets the rent

Rent ≈ £2,490 + £0.57 × sq ft − £8 × minutes to Bank
An extra 100 sq ft adds about £57 a month. Each minute closer to Bank adds about £8 a month.

Size and commute explain only a small part of the price: a typical flat is £255 away from what this formula predicts. The building and the listing matter more. So choose the district for its commute, then compare flats on the same streets.

    Before you decide

    • This is one week of asking rents. The figures firm up after 4–6 weekly runs.
    • The E1 typical rent is £238 (8.6%) below your cap. ONS two-bed rents in Tower Hamlets rose 3.66% in the year to September 2026.
    • Shoreditch's Chequer Street flat (EC1Y) is the best value at 18 minutes on foot. It costs the full £3,000, so it leaves no room for a rise.
    • Four listings give impossible floor areas (1–71 sq ft). They are left out of every £ per sq ft figure.
    • TfL times use each flat's location rounded to about 100 m. Walking time to the station can differ by a few minutes.
    • Every DLR route ends with TfL's 9-minute walk from Bank DLR to your office, which covers 307 of the 625 timed flats. Google Maps, through Gemini, gave 3 minutes. If that walk is 4 minutes shorter, Canary Wharf gains 44 flats within 30 minutes (51 to 95), Wapping gains 7, and Royal Docks goes from 0 to 23. E1 does not change. Time the walk yourself once, from the DLR platform to the door.
    • Some build-to-rent flats can still be missing. The Vertus, Greystar, Moda and Uncle pages could not be fully checked.

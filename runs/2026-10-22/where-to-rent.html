    What sets the rent

    This fits one equation to the 260 flats that have both a floor area and a commute time:

    Rent ≈ £2,490 + £0.57 × sq ft − £8 × minutes to Bank
    An extra 100 sq ft adds about £57 a month. Each minute closer to Bank adds about £8 a month.

    The equation is weak. Size and commute together explain only 12% of the differences in rent, and a typical flat sits £255 away from its prediction. Your £3,000 cap packs every rent into a narrow band, so the building and the listing matter more than any formula. Knowing the district explains a little more than size and commute, but most of the spread stays unexplained:

    Before you decide

    • This is one week of asking rents. Prices and medians firm up after 4–6 weekly runs.
    • Room to grow: the E1 median is £238 below your cap, which is 8.6%. The ONS figure for two-bed rents in Tower Hamlets rose 3.66% in the year to September 2026. At that rate the headroom lasts about two years, which is close to late 2027.
    • Shoreditch's Chequer Street flat (EC1Y) is the best value at 18 minutes on foot. It costs the full £3,000, so it leaves no room for a rise.
    • Four listings give impossible floor areas (1–71 sq ft). They are left out of every £/sq ft figure.
    • TfL times use each flat's coordinates rounded to about 100 m. Walking time to the station can differ by a few minutes.
    • Every DLR route ends with TfL's 9-minute walk from Bank DLR to your office, which covers 297 of the 615 timed flats. Google Maps, through Gemini, gave 3 minutes. The DLR platforms are deep, so the true figure is probably in between. If that walk is 4 minutes shorter, Canary Wharf gains 44 more flats within 30 minutes (51 to 95), Wapping gains 7, and Royal Docks goes from 0 to 13. E1 does not change. Time the walk yourself once, from the DLR platform to the door.
    • The tracker misses some build-to-rent flats. A second price series found 6 Fizzy units in E16 (Vermilion Tower and East 16) at £2,409–£2,885, and the tracker has none of them. It dropped 13 Fizzy and 31 Tipi two-bed units because their pages give no postcode, yet still marked those searches OK.
